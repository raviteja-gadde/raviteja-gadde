## Ravi Teja Gadde

I'm a software engineer, ten years in, and I like to own the whole problem: design, code, the infrastructure it runs on, and what happens after it ships. Most of that time has been in analytics platforms, payments, and financial services, where mistakes cost money.

What I care about most is that things hold up. I'd rather understand a system down to the layer where it can fail than trust an abstraction I can't reason about. For an AI product that means the layers underneath it: the data it stands on, the identity and access around it, the retrieval that feeds it, and the evaluation that says whether it works.

The next stretch of my work is agent systems: giving models tools, boundaries, and enough evaluation to trust them with real decisions. My day job is building exactly that at Gallup, where I took Gallup AI from first prototype to production. The architecture is in the AWS Architecture Blog post I co-authored, [Gallup delivers real-time workplace coaching to thousands of leaders with Amazon Bedrock](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/architecture/gallup-delivers-real-time-workplace-coaching-to-thousands-of-leaders-with-amazon-bedrock/).

More at [raviteja-gadde.github.io](https://raviteja-gadde.github.io). Greater Phoenix Area.
