## Ravi Teja Gadde

Senior Software Engineer on the Gallup AI team, working on the shared foundation the Gallup AI products are built on: retrieval, agents, evaluation, and the production systems around them on AWS, from RFC to running in production. One of those systems is described in the AWS Architecture Blog post I co-authored: [Gallup delivers real-time workplace coaching to thousands of leaders with Amazon Bedrock](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/architecture/gallup-delivers-real-time-workplace-coaching-to-thousands-of-leaders-with-amazon-bedrock/).

Ten years building products end to end across analytics platforms, payment systems, and financial services. The repos here go deep on the layers an AI product actually stands on: data, identity, retrieval, evaluation.
